I'm Aryan, a visual artist, photographer, and filmmaker based in Toronto, Canada. Online, I go by the name SlumberViz across YouTube, Instagram, and all my social platforms.

I’m drawn to visual art in all forms – from still frames to motion – and I love exploring how light, color, and composition can shape a feeling. My work is inspired by sunsets, beaches, and the energy of the city.

From drone perspectives to intimate close-ups, I’m always chasing visuals that feel honest and memorable. If you’re in Toronto or nearby and want to build something together, I’d love to hear what you have in mind.
